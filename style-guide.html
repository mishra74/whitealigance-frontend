05 — Motion

Signature: The Thread Line

A single hairline in Soft Gold draws itself across headings and dividers — a quiet stitch motif that echoes fabric and tailoring. It recurs everywhere: under section titles, beneath nav links on hover, and at the center of the hero→Section 2 dupatta-unfold transition. One gesture, used with restraint, becomes the signature the whole site is remembered by.

ease-fabric
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
duration-quick
200ms — hovers, taps
duration-base
400ms — buttons, nav underline
duration-reveal
900ms — scroll reveals, mask-ins
